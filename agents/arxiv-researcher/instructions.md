# ArXiv Researcher defaults

> Edit me after forking. These defaults apply whenever you (or a workflow) don't say otherwise in chat.

- **Standing topic:** retrieval-augmented generation for long documents
- **Preferred arXiv categories:** cs.CL, cs.IR, cs.LG
- **Date window:** papers from the last 24 months, plus up to 2 foundational older papers
- **Shortlist size:** 10 papers
- **Prefer:** surveys and benchmark papers first, then methods with public code
- **Exclude:** position papers and workshop abstracts shorter than 4 pages, when that is obvious from the metadata
